Ten feet (3048 mm) or more from lot lines and building or structure openings.

fire
-resistance-rated assembly or buildings or structures having combustible overhangs.

Twenty feet (6096 mm) or more from fixed sources of ignition.

exit
.

## Section 2304.1.2: Emergency Fuel Shutoff

2304.1.2 Emergency Fuel Shutoff
An
approved
, clearly identified and readily accessible emergency fuel shutoff switch shall be provided at an
approved
location to immediately shut down the transfer of fuel to the fuel dispensers in the event of a fuel spill or other emergency and activate an alarm audible in the
dispensing
area and any control booth. Such audible device may be the same device used to indicate activation of the
fire extinguishing system
installed to protect the fuel dispensers. An emergency fuel shutoff switch for outdoor fuel dispensers shall be located within 100 feet (30 480 mm) of, but not less than 20 feet (6096 mm) from, the fuel dispensers. For
indoor
fuel dispensers, the emergency fuel shutoff switch shall be installed at an
approved
location. An
approved
sign shall be posted on or immediately adjacent to the emergency fuel shutoff switch, which reads: EMERGENCY FUEL SHUTOFF. Such emergency fuel shutoff switches shall be of a type that must be reset manually.
