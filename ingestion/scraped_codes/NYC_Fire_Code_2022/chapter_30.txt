Section 3001 General

---

## Section 3001.1: Scope

3001.1 Scope
This chapter shall govern the design, installation, operation and
maintenance
of industrial
furnaces
used for commercial and industrial processing of materials.

---

This chapter shall govern the design, installation, operation and
maintenance
of industrial
furnaces
used for commercial and industrial processing of materials.

---

## Section 3001.2: Permits

3001.2 Permits
Permits
shall be required as set forth in
FC105.6
.

---

Permits
shall be required as set forth in
FC105.6
.

---

## Section 3001.3: General

3001.3 General
Industrial
furnaces
shall be designed, installed, operated and maintained in accordance with this chapter, NFPA 86, and the
construction codes
, including the
Fuel Gas Code
and the
Mechanical Code
.

---

Industrial
furnaces
shall be designed, installed, operated and maintained in accordance with this chapter, NFPA 86, and the
construction codes
, including the
Fuel Gas Code
and the
Mechanical Code
.

Section 3003 Location

---

## Section 3003.1: Ventilation

3003.1 Ventilation
Enclosed rooms, basements or other areas below grade containing industrial
furnaces
shall be provided with combustion air in accordance with the
construction codes
, including the
Mechanical Code
and the
Fuel Gas Code
, and with
ventilation
in accordance with the
Mechanical Code
.

---

Enclosed rooms, basements or other areas below grade containing industrial
furnaces
shall be provided with combustion air in accordance with the
construction codes
, including the
Mechanical Code
and the
Fuel Gas Code
, and with
ventilation
in accordance with the
Mechanical Code
.

---

## Section 3003.2: Location

3003.2 Location
Industrial
furnaces
and heaters shall be located such that in the event of
fire
or
explosion
resulting from overheating or from the escape of fuel gas or fuel oil, damage to the building and injury to persons is minimized.

---

Industrial
furnaces
and heaters shall be located such that in the event of
fire
or
explosion
resulting from overheating or from the escape of fuel gas or fuel oil, damage to the building and injury to persons is minimized.

---

## Section 3003.3: Ignition Source

3003.3 Ignition Source
Industrial
furnaces
shall be located so as not to pose an ignition hazard to flammable vapors or mists or
combustible dusts
that may be present as a result of a manufacturing operation or other lawful use and
occupancy
of the building.

---

Industrial
furnaces
shall be located so as not to pose an ignition hazard to flammable vapors or mists or
combustible dusts
that may be present as a result of a manufacturing operation or other lawful use and
occupancy
of the building.

---

## Section 3003.4: Temperatures

3003.4 Temperatures
Roofs and floors of
furnaces
shall be insulated and ventilated to prevent temperatures at combustible ceilings and floors from exceeding 160°F (71°C).

---

Roofs and floors of
furnaces
shall be insulated and ventilated to prevent temperatures at combustible ceilings and floors from exceeding 160°F (71°C).

Section 3004 Fuel Piping

---

## Section 3004.1: Fuel-Gas Piping

3004.1 Fuel-Gas Piping
Fuel-gas piping serving industrial
furnaces
shall be designed and installed in accordance with the
Fuel Gas Code
. Piping for other fuel sources shall comply with the requirements of this section.

---

Fuel-gas piping serving industrial
furnaces
shall be designed and installed in accordance with the
Fuel Gas Code
. Piping for other fuel sources shall comply with the requirements of this section.

---

## Section 3004.2: Shutoff Valves

3004.2 Shutoff Valves
Each industrial
furnace
shall be provided with an
approved
manual fuel shutoff valve in accordance with the
construction codes
, including the
Mechanical Code
and the
Fuel Gas Code
.

---

Each industrial
furnace
shall be provided with an
approved
manual fuel shutoff valve in accordance with the
construction codes
, including the
Mechanical Code
and the
Fuel Gas Code
.

---

## Section 3004.2.1: Fuel Supply Lines

3004.2.1 Fuel Supply Lines
Valves for fuel supply lines shall be located within 6 feet (1829 mm) of the appliance served.
Exception:
When
approved
and the valve is located in the same
general
area as the appliance served.

---

Valves for fuel supply lines shall be located within 6 feet (1829 mm) of the appliance served.
Exception:
When
approved
and the valve is located in the same
general
area as the appliance served.

---

## Section 3004.3: Valve Position

3004.3 Valve Position
The design of manual fuel shutoff valves shall incorporate a permanent feature that visually indicates the open or closed position of the valve. Manual fuel shutoff valves shall not be equipped with removable handles or wrenches unless the handle or wrench can only be installed parallel with the fuel line when the valve is in the open position.

---

The design of manual fuel shutoff valves shall incorporate a permanent feature that visually indicates the open or closed position of the valve. Manual fuel shutoff valves shall not be equipped with removable handles or wrenches unless the handle or wrench can only be installed parallel with the fuel line when the valve is in the open position.

---

## Section 3005.1: Shut Down

3005.1 Shut Down
Interlocks shall be provided for Class A
furnaces
so that conveyors or sources of flammable or combustible materials shall shut down if either the exhaust or recirculation air supply fails.

---

Interlocks shall be provided for Class A
furnaces
so that conveyors or sources of flammable or combustible materials shall shut down if either the exhaust or recirculation air supply fails.

Section 3006 Fire Protection

---

## Section 3006.1: Required Protection

3006.1 Required Protection
Class A and B
furnaces
that contain, or are utilized for the processing of, combustible materials shall be protected throughout by a
fire extinguishing system
complying with the requirements of
FC Chapter 9
.

---

Class A and B
furnaces
that contain, or are utilized for the processing of, combustible materials shall be protected throughout by a
fire extinguishing system
complying with the requirements of
FC Chapter 9
.

---

## Section 3006.2: Fixed Fire Extinguishing Systems

3006.2 Fixed Fire Extinguishing Systems
A fixed
fire extinguishing system
shall be provided for Class C or D
furnaces
to protect against such hazards as overheating, spillage of molten salts or metals, quench
tanks
, ignition of hydraulic oil and escape of fuel.

---

A fixed
fire extinguishing system
shall be provided for Class C or D
furnaces
to protect against such hazards as overheating, spillage of molten salts or metals, quench
tanks
, ignition of hydraulic oil and escape of fuel.

---

## Section 3006.3: Portable Fire Extinguishers

3006.3 Portable Fire Extinguishers
Portable
fire
extinguishers complying with the requirements of
FC906
shall be provided not closer than 15 feet (4572 mm) or a maximum of 50 feet (15 240 mm) from each
furnace
or in accordance with NFPA 10.

---

Portable
fire
extinguishers complying with the requirements of
FC906
shall be provided not closer than 15 feet (4572 mm) or a maximum of 50 feet (15 240 mm) from each
furnace
or in accordance with NFPA 10.

furnaces
