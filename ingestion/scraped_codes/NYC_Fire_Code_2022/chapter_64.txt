Pyrophoric materials

.

## Section 6403.1.1.1: Shutoff at Source

A manual or
automatic
fail-safe
emergency shutoff valve
shall be installed on supply piping at the
container
or other source of supply. The shutoff valve shall be operated by a remotely located manually activated shutdown control located not less than 15 feet (4572 mm) from the source of supply. Manual or
automatic
container
valves on the
containers
supplying the
pyrophoric material
may serve as the
emergency shutoff valve
when the
containers
are the sole source of supply and are not manifolded.
