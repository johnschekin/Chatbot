Section 6501 General

---

## Section 6501.1: Scope

6501.1 Scope
This chapter shall govern the manufacture, storage,
handling
and use of
pyroxylin plastic
, except cellulose nitrate motion picture film, which shall comply with the requirements of
FC306
.

---

This chapter shall govern the manufacture, storage,
handling
and use of
pyroxylin plastic
, except cellulose nitrate motion picture film, which shall comply with the requirements of
FC306

---

shall be required as set forth in

## Section 6501.3: General

6501.3 General
Pyroxylin plastics

Section 6503 General Requirements

---

## Section 6503.1: Displays

6503.1 Displays
Finished pyroxylin plastic products
displayed in areas to which the public has access shall be placed on tables not more than 3 feet (914 mm) wide and 10 feet (3048 mm) long. Tables shall be spaced at least 3 feet (914 mm) apart. Where articles are displayed on counters, they shall be arranged in a like manner.

---

Finished pyroxylin plastic products
displayed in areas to which the public has access shall be placed on tables not more than 3 feet (914 mm) wide and 10 feet (3048 mm) long. Tables shall be spaced at least 3 feet (914 mm) apart. Where articles are displayed on counters, they shall be arranged in a like manner.

---

## Section 6503.2: Space Under Tables

6503.2 Space Under Tables
Spaces underneath tables on which
finished pyroxylin plastic products
are displayed shall be kept free from storage of any kind and accumulation of
rubbish
or other
combustible waste
or any other combustible material.

---

Spaces underneath tables on which
finished pyroxylin plastic products
are displayed shall be kept free from storage of any kind and accumulation of
rubbish
or other
combustible waste
or any other combustible material.

---

## Section 6503.3: Location

6503.3 Location
Tables on which
finished pyroxylin plastic products
are displayed shall be so located that in the event of a
fire
at the table, the table will not interfere with any
means of egress
.

---

Tables on which
finished pyroxylin plastic products
are displayed shall be so located that in the event of a
fire
at the table, the table will not interfere with any
means of egress
.

---

## Section 6503.4: Lighting

6503.4 Lighting
Lighting
shall not be located directly above
pyroxylin plastics
, unless provided with a suitable guard to prevent heated particles or broken bulb elements from falling.

---

Lighting
shall not be located directly above
pyroxylin plastics
, unless provided with a suitable guard to prevent heated particles or broken bulb elements from falling.

Section 6504 Storage, Handling and Use

---

## Section 6504.1: Pyroxylin Plastics in Group F Buildings

6504.1 Pyroxylin Plastics in Group F Buildings
Pyroxylin plastics
in a
Group F
building shall be stored, handled and used in accordance with
FC 6504.1.1
through
6504.1.7
.

---

Pyroxylin plastics
in a
Group F
building shall be stored, handled and used in accordance with
FC 6504.1.1
through
6504.1.7
.

---

## Section 6504.1.1: Storage of Incoming Material

6504.1.1 Storage of Incoming Material
Where
raw pyroxylin plastics
in excess of 25 pounds (11 kg) are received in a building or
fire area
, an
approved
vented cabinet or
approved
vented vault protected by a
sprinkler system
shall be provided for the storage of material.

---

Where
raw pyroxylin plastics
in excess of 25 pounds (11 kg) are received in a building or
fire area
, an
approved
vented cabinet or
approved
vented vault protected by a
sprinkler system
shall be provided for the storage of material.

---

## Section 6504.1.2: Capacity Limitations

6504.1.2 Capacity Limitations
Storage in any one workroom shall not exceed 1,000 pounds (454 kg) of
raw pyroxylin plastics
. A storage cabinet shall not contain more than 500 pounds (227 kg). Each compartment shall not contain more than 250 pounds (114 kg).

---

Storage in any one workroom shall not exceed 1,000 pounds (454 kg) of
raw pyroxylin plastics
. A storage cabinet shall not contain more than 500 pounds (227 kg). Each compartment shall not contain more than 250 pounds (114 kg).

---

## Section 6504.1.3: Storage of Additional Material

6504.1.3 Storage of Additional Material
Raw pyroxylin plastics
in excess of that allowed by
FC6504.1.2
shall be kept in vented vaults not exceeding 1,500-cubic-foot capacity (43 m
3
) of total vault space, and with construction, venting and
sprinkler system
protection
approved
by the
commissioner
.

---

Raw pyroxylin plastics
in excess of that allowed by
FC6504.1.2

3
) of total vault space, and with construction, venting and
sprinkler system
protection
approved
by the
commissioner

---

## Section 6504.1.4: Heat Sources

6504.1.4 Heat Sources
Pyroxylin plastics
shall not be stored within 2 feet (610 mm) of
heat-producing appliances
, steam pipes, radiators or chimneys.

---

Pyroxylin plastics
shall not be stored within 2 feet (610 mm) of
heat-producing appliances
, steam pipes, radiators or chimneys.

---

pyroxylin plastic
products,
approved
vented cabinets, vaults or storage rooms protected by a
sprinkler system
shall be provided to prevent the accumulation in workrooms of
raw pyroxylin plastics
in process and
finished pyroxylin plastic products
.

---

In factories manufacturing
pyroxylin plastic

## Section 6504.1.6: Operators

6504.1.6 Operators
In workrooms of factories manufacturing
finished pyroxylin plastic products
, operators shall not be stationed closer together than 3 feet (914 mm), and the amount of material per operator shall not exceed one-shift's supply and shall be limited to the capacity of three tote boxes, including material awaiting removal or use.

in a quantity not exceeding 6,000 pounds (2724 kg).

## Section 6504.3: Sources of Ignition

6504.3 Sources of Ignition
Sources of ignition shall not be allowed in rooms in which
pyroxylin plastics
in excess of 25 pounds (11 kg) are stored, handled or used.

---

Sources of ignition shall not be allowed in rooms in which

## Section 6504.4: Heating

6504.4 Heating
Rooms in which
pyroxylin plastics
are handled or stored shall be heated by low-pressure steam or hot water radiators. Radiators shall be not less than 4 inches (102 mm) above the floor. All steam pipes and risers within 6 feet (1829 mm) of the floor shall be protected with noncombustible pipe covering. All heating radiators, coils, boilers, pipes and other heating apparatus situated so as to come in contact with any combustible material, including the tops of radiators, shall be protected with
1
/
4
-inch (6-mm) mesh galvanized
steel
of No. 20 U.S. gauge or equivalent. The tops of such guards shall slope so as to prevent their use as a shelf.

---
