manner.

## Section 5308.5.3: Vent Lines

5308.5.3 Vent Lines
Sterilizer vent lines shall be designed and installed in compliance with the following requirements:
Each sterilizer shall be equipped with an independent, dedicated and gas-tight vent line for the discharge of gases containing ethylene oxide.
Vent lines shall discharge directly outdoors or to an emission control
system
. Such vent lines shall not discharge into any other
ventilation
or exhaust
system
.
The piping and the point of discharge for all vent lines discharging outdoors shall be designed and installed to prevent moisture from entering the vent line.
Vent lines, including piping, fittings and other components, shall be in accordance with the specifications of the sterilizer manufacturer. Pipe lengths shall not exceed the maximum lengths specified by the manufacturer. Vertical travel distances, elbows, sharp bends and any reduction in vent line size shall be kept to a minimum.
