## Section 5806.3.5: Vent Lines

5806.3.5 Vent Lines
Sterilization
system
vent lines shall be designed and installed in accordance with the following requirements:
The discharge point of vent lines shall be at least 25 feet (7620 mm) from pedestrian traffic, building openings and sources of ignition. Prevailing wind direction and location of adjacent buildings shall be considered in selecting the discharge point.
At each discharge location there shall be a durable sign, conspicuously posted, that reads "Danger—
Flammable Gas
."
Signage shall appear on the vent line at intervals of not more than 20 feet (6096 mm) and shall be present in at least one place in each room and in each story traversed by the duct.
A sparkproof centrifugal fan with backward curved blades designed for continuous operation shall be used, and the impeller and the ring around the impeller drive shaft shall be nonferrous.
Vent terminals shall be provided with a flash arrester, provided that the manufacturer of the sterilizer or the testing laboratory does not prohibit same. The flash arrester, when used, shall be constructed of material compatible with ethylene oxide and installed in such a manner as not to restrict gas flow.
