## Section 6001.4: Highly Toxic and Toxic Material Mixtures

6001.4 Highly Toxic and Toxic Material Mixtures
The level of toxicity of highly toxic and
toxic materials
may be reduced by diluting such materials with other materials, such as water, to a degree that the resulting mixture may no longer be highly toxic or toxic. A mixture containing any amount of highly toxic and/or
toxic material
is presumed to be a highly toxic or
toxic material
, as applicable, unless it is otherwise certified and
labeled
by the manufacturer.
