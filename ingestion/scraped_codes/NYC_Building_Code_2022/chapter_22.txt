Section BC 2203 Identification and Protection of Steel for Structural Purposes

---

## Section 2203.1: Identification

2203.1 Identification
Identification of
structural steel elements
shall be in accordance with the requirements contained in AISC 303. Identification of cold-formed steel members shall be in accordance with the requirements contained in AISI S100. Identification of cold-formed steel
light-frame construction
shall also comply with the requirements contained in AISI S240 or AISI S220, as applicable. Other steel furnished for structural load-carrying purposes shall be properly identified for conformity to the ordered grade in accordance with the specified ASTM standard or other specification and the provisions of this chapter. Where the steel grade is not readily identifiable from marking and test records, the steel shall be tested to determine conformity to such standards.
