building
is equipped throughout with an
automatic
fire sprinkler system in accordance with
Section 903.3.1.1
. The approved
automatic sprinkler system
shall be provided in both the room and that part of the
building
in which the room is located.

,

or

shall not be limited for roof
applications
.
Foam plastic insulation

flame spread index
of 75 and a

using the thickness and density intended for use.

## Section 2603.4: Thermal Barrier

2603.4 Thermal Barrier
Except as provided for in Sections
2603.4.1
and
2603.9
, foam
plastic
shall be separated from the interior of a
building
by an approved thermal
barrier
of
1
/
2
-inch (12.7 mm) gypsum wallboard or equivalent thermal barrier material that will limit the average temperature rise of the unexposed surface to not more than 250°F (120°C) after 15 minutes of fire exposure, complying with the standard time-temperature curve of ASTM E 119 or UL 263. The thermal barrier shall be installed in such a manner that it will remain in place for 15 minutes based on FM 4880, UL 1040, NFPA 286 or UL 1715. Combustible
concealed spaces
shall comply with Section 718‡ of this code.
Exception:
Thermal barriers for
exterior walls
shall comply with
Section 2603.5.2
.

automatic sprinkler system
in accordance with
Section 903.3.1.1
. Where the cooler or freezer is within a
building
, both the cooler or freezer and that part of the
building
in which it is located shall be sprinklered.
