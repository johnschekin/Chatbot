Section 703 General Requirements

---

## Section 703.1: Hydrogen Generation

703.1 Hydrogen Generation
The generation of gaseous hydrogen for immediate on-premises use
in indoor fuel cells or other energy production process and incidental indoor storage of gaseous
hydrogen shall be located in accordance with
Section 706
.
Exhaust
ventilation shall be required in
rooms or spaces that house such generation of gaseous hydrogen in accordance with the applicable
provisions of NFPA 2.
