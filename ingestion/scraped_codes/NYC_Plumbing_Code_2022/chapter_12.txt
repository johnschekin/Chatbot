Chapter 12 Special Piping and Storage Systems

---

Section 1201 General

---

## Section 1201.1: Scope

1201.1 Scope
The provisions of this chapter shall govern the design and installation of piping and storage systems for nonflammable medical and
nonmedical gas systems
.

---

The provisions of this chapter shall govern the design and installation of piping and storage systems for nonflammable medical and
nonmedical gas systems
.

---

## Section 1201.2: Storage, Handling, and Use

1201.2 Storage, Handling, and Use
The storage, handling, and use of medical and nonmedical gases shall be in accordance with the
New York City Fire Code
.

---

The storage, handling, and use of medical and nonmedical gases shall be in accordance with the
New York City Fire Code
.

Section 1202 Medical and Nonmedical Gases

---

## Section 1202.1: Nonflammable Medical and Nonmedical Gases

1202.1 Nonflammable Medical and Nonmedical Gases
Nonflammable medical and
nonmedical gas systems
shall be designed and installed in accordance with NFPA 99.
Exceptions:
This section shall not apply to portable systems or cylinder storage.
Vacuum
system exhaust terminations shall comply with the
New York City Mechanical Code
.

---

Section 1203 Nonmedical Oxygen Systems

---

## Section 1203.1: Design and Installation

1203.1 Design and Installation
Nonmedical oxygen systems shall be designed and installed in accordance with NFPA 55 and NFPA 51.

---

Nonmedical oxygen systems shall be designed and installed in accordance with NFPA 55 and NFPA 51.
