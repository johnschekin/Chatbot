New York City Building Code
.

noncombustible materials

Exceptions:
Fill and drift eliminators of
limited combustible materials
may be considered noncombustible if the cooling towers are provided with automatic sprinkler
protection
in compliance with
Chapter 9
of the
New York City Building Code
.
Fill and drift eliminators for any individual cooling tower cell may be made of
limited combustible materials

The cooling tower is located on a building of construction Types IA or IB of the
New York City Building Code

The cooling tower cell is located at least 30 feet (9144 mm) away from windows or fresh air intakes which are at an elevation above the roof on which the cooling tower is located, whether in the same building or in an adjoining building.

chimney

venting products of
combustion
other than from gas- or
