## Section 1001.1: Scope

1001.1 Scope
This chapter shall establish the minimum safety requirements for and shall govern the
installation
, alteration and
repair
of boilers,
water heaters
and
pressure vessels
.
Exceptions:
Pressure vessels
used for unheated water supply.
Portable unfired
pressure vessels
and Interstate Commerce Commission containers.
Containers for bulk oxygen and medical gas.
Unfired
pressure vessels
having a volume of 5 cubic feet (0.14 m
3
) or less operating at pressures not exceeding 250 pounds per square inch (psi) (1723.7 kPa) and located within occupancies of Groups B, F, H, M, R, S and U.
Pressure vessels
used in refrigeration systems that are regulated by
Chapter 11
.
Pressure tanks used in conjunction with coaxial cables, telephone cables, power cables and other similar humidity
control
systems.
Any
boiler
or pressure vessel subject to inspection by federal inspectors.

Section 607
of the
New York City Plumbing Code
